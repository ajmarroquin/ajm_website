## Star Wars Unlimited

Fantasy Flight Games' Star Wars card game, out since 2024. You pick a leader and a base, and two players race to destroy each other's base, fighting across a ground arena and a space arena.

I also keep the [[NYC Star Wars Unlimited Events]] page, so NYC players can find weekly nights and tournaments.

## Sorcery: Contested Realm

A fantasy card game out since 2023, known for its old-school, hand-painted art. Instead of cards in rows, you build out a grid-shaped realm and move your units across it.

## Disney Lorcana

Ravensburger's Disney card game, out since 2023. Disney characters are your cards, and players race to be first to collect 20 lore.
